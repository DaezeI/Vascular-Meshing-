How Do We Turn a Medical Scan Into a Blood-Flow Grid?

A segmented CT scan gives clinicians a hollow triangle shell of a patient's arteries. To simulate blood flow and wall stress, engineers must fill that interior volume with a valid 3D mesh.

Methodology, Citations & Glossary

Sources & Quality Metrics

2D Gamma (γ ∈ [0,1])

Triangle regularity. Near 1.0 is equilateral; near 0 is a flat sliver.

minSICN (≤ 1)

Jacobian orientation. Any value ≤ 0 is an inverted cell that halts FE/CFD solvers.

minSIGE (≤ 1)

Solution gradient fidelity for wall shear stress; closer to 1.0 is cleaner.
